## Skill scout

At a natural stopping point in user-facing work, if the user has confirmed a durable, reusable, non-obvious procedure they would otherwise explain again, load the `skill-scout` skill and follow it. Otherwise stay silent; most sessions need no suggestion.

- Propose in at most two sentences. Never create, edit, or delete a skill without the user's explicit approval of that specific change.
- Do not apply this in subagents, title generation, or summarization.
- When summarizing or compacting context, keep pending skill proposals, the user's decisions, and rejected topics. If approval is unclear afterwards, ask.
